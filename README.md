# Remember Twelve

**Preserve your year in twelve unforgettable moments — for every circle that matters.**

An AI-powered memory preservation app that automatically curates twelve meaningful photos per year for the people who matter most. Family. Kids. Extended family. Each circle gets its own twelve.

Built in October 2025 — one of my earlier projects, and it actually works.

---

## What it does

Most photos disappear into cloud storage never to be seen again. Remember Twelve changes that by automatically selecting twelve photos per year — the ones that actually capture the year — and organizing them by the people in your life.

- **AI-powered curation** — intelligently selects the twelve most meaningful photos from your library, not just the most recent or the most liked
- **Multi-circle organization** — separate archives for Family, Individual, Kids, Extended Family, and any circle you define
- **Apple Photos integration** — works with your existing photo library
- **Reflection artifacts** — creates lasting, revisitable memory archives designed to hold up for decades

---

## The philosophy

> *Preservation over perfection. Automatic curation beats manual perfection that never happens.*

Most people intend to make photo books. Almost nobody actually does. Remember Twelve removes the friction entirely — zero configuration required, with full override when you want it.
